# Controls

## Firearms

- **Left mouse button:** fire.
- **Right mouse button:** aim.
- **R:** reload.
- **B:** change fire mode on weapons with a selector.
- **Alt + movement:** slow walk; rebind the key in Minecraft controls if needed.
- **Ctrl + movement:** run; **Shift:** crouch (vanilla bindings).

## 1812 muskets and pistols

- Muskets use `paper_cartridge`; pistols use `pistol_cartridge`.
- One cartridge is one shot. These weapons have no detachable magazine.
- Loading takes 8 seconds for a musket and 6 seconds for a pistol.
- Sabres use Minecraft's normal melee controls.

## 1812 field guns

1. Place the cannon with right-click on a clear surface.
2. Right-click it with an empty hand to aim. Hold **Shift** while right-clicking to adjust elevation.
3. Load the matching charge: `charge_fr6` for the French 6-pounder or `charge_ri12` for the Russian 12-pounder. They are not interchangeable.
4. Wait for loading to finish, then use flint and steel on the cannon to fire.

The charge includes a solid projectile. It follows a ballistic path and deals direct impact damage; it is not an explosive shell.

## AI soldiers

- Spawn: `/realwar npc squad <faction> <era> [count] [vehicle]` (see `docs/npc-soldiers.md`), or use the spawn eggs
  in the **Troops** creative tabs.
- AI soldiers need no keys: they pick targets, hold their squad, crew their own nation's vehicles and fly their own
  aircraft. Only the player-driven seats use the controls below.
- Killed soldiers drop a partly loaded weapon and a damaged uniform; search the body with the normal item pickup.

## Vehicles and aircraft

Mount the vehicle and press **J** (default) to open its contextual control panel. It displays the controls available for that vehicle; rebind keys in **Options → Controls → AI Arsenal** if needed.

## Updating a world

Back up the world before replacing the mod JAR. Keep the same mod ID and avoid installing two versions at once.
